While Loop

Repeats while condition is true.

while condition:

Examples:

i=i+1 → increment
i=i-1 → decrement
Conditions in Loop
if i%2==0

→ even numbers

if i%3==0 and i%5==0

→ divisible by both 3 and 5.

Nested Loop

Loop inside another loop.

while i<=5:
    while j<=4:

Used for:

patterns
repeated rows/columns

range(start,stop,step)

Examples:

range(10)
range(1,10)
range(1,10,3)
